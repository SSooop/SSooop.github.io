# IntelliPharma Hub house style

## Authorial position

Write as an AIDD practitioner who uses cross-disciplinary theory to rename a real industry problem, not as a neutral AI explainer. Begin by acknowledging the genuine value of a technology or commercial development, then identify what its current frame cannot explain. Critique evaluation systems, problem definitions, and organizational logic more often than individual companies.

External concepts from control theory, economics, systems engineering, history, or philosophy must do real analytical work. They should be forced into the essay by an AIDD problem, tested against concrete research or industry decisions, and returned to practice. If removing a theorist or framework leaves the conclusion unchanged, it is decoration and should be cut.

The stable movement is often:

1. A current event, personal observation, historical scene, or concrete contradiction.
2. The conflict hidden by the usual description.
3. A theoretical tool introduced for one specific job.
4. A case, historical episode, or evidence that tests the tool.
5. An explicit return to AIDD, experiments, organizations, or patient value.
6. Counterexamples and scope boundaries.
7. A new practical judgment derived from the argument.
8. A conclusion that closes the opening loop.

Do not expose this as a mechanical template.

## Structure and rhythm

- Prefer continuous argument for philosophical or opinion essays. Outline headings are scaffolding, not default published headings. Use no headings or at most 3–5 headings that genuinely change the level of argument.
- Use lists only for true classifications, design principles, or exact comparisons. Carry the main argument in paragraphs.
- Let each paragraph advance one judgment and show its causal or contrastive relationship to the previous paragraph. Typical Chinese paragraphs are 2–5 sentences and roughly 120–200 characters.
- Single-sentence paragraphs can mark a turn, question, or earned conclusion, but do not stack them.
- Long sentences are part of the voice. Follow a dense abstraction with a concrete case or a short judgment instead of piling up clauses.
- For Chinese, default to roughly 3,500–5,000 characters unless `style.md` specifies otherwise; argumentative completeness matters more than a quota.

## Tone and language

- Hold a clear position without sounding omniscient. Use “我” or “笔者” only for a real experience, motive, or explicitly stated view. Use “我们” to invite the reader through an inference.
- Steelman an approach before defining its boundary. The criticism should be pointed but not mocking or tribal.
- Allow an earned memorable antithesis or concept label, but ration patterns such as “不是 A，而是 B”. Do not turn every section into a symmetric slogan.
- Avoid ungrounded bundles such as “系统性、结构性、底层逻辑、认知边界、价值密度、范式重构”. Every abstraction must answer what concretely changes.
- Do not overuse “核心”, “真正”, “本质上”, “并非”, “而是”, or stock transitions. Avoid “首先、其次、最后” as a textbook skeleton.
- Avoid generic AI prose: “在这个快速变化的时代”, repetitive three-part symmetry, a slogan at every paragraph end, and grand historical optimism in place of analysis.
- A question should open a problem the article actually answers, not manufacture intensity.

## Terminology and evidence

- Use English in Chinese prose only when it is the field's native term or translation loses precision. On first use prefer “中文（English term, ABBR）”; keep later spelling and case consistent. Put spaces between Chinese and English or numbers.
- Define a coined term operationally and keep its meaning stable.
- A historical episode must bear argumentative weight, not merely provide a grand opening.
- Test every major theory with at least one realistic AIDD decision. State analogy boundaries explicitly: an engineering architecture may give a useful intuition without formally proving a philosophy of science.
- Verify dates, numbers, direct quotations, and product capabilities with primary or official sources. Keep citations near the supported claim. A source proves a fact, not automatically the author's inference.
- Do not add naked URLs, placeholder citations, hand-written citation modules, or unsupported publication surfaces to the body.
- Never invent the author's experience, employer practices, tools, benchmarks, or feelings.

## Endings

Return to the opening event, scene, or concept and answer how the analysis changes what should be understood, evaluated, or done next. Idealism may appear only when earned by the preceding reasoning. Avoid “让我们拭目以待”, “一切只是时间问题”, “历史的车轮滚滚向前”, and other endings that add no judgment.

## English version

Write an independent English essay with the same argument, evidence, and level of qualification. Rewrite by paragraph rather than preserving Chinese sentence order. Translate cultural idioms by function, define acronyms again, shorten overnested Chinese sentences, and avoid repeated calques such as “the essence lies in” or “it is precisely”. A single Chinese character or Chinese punctuation mark remaining in the English body means the pass is incomplete.
