# AIDD's Breakthrough in Heavy Asset Industries: Lessons from Palantir

The first half of 2025 saw significant growth in AI Agent applications, with AI Coding achieving substantial commercial breakthroughs. According to publicly available data, Cursor reached $500 million in Annual Recurring Revenue (ARR). This differentiated performance stems from objective factors: AI Agent applications in non-programming domains still face real-world scenario adaptation challenges, while programming scenarios offer commercializable foundations including standardized tasks, quantifiable ROI, and clear willingness to pay.

From an industry perspective, Coding as a typical light-asset industry has its business model validated in some traditional internet+ domains. Based on current technology evolution paths, AI commercialization in e-commerce, content generation, advertising, and education may accelerate in the second half of the year. However, the applicability of this model in heavy asset industries remains to be observed. Palantir's (NASDAQ: PLTR) performance in the capital market last year may provide a reference case for this question.

Palantir's core technological advantages are generally recognized in two aspects: its Ontology architecture system and vertical integration capabilities. The company's business practice shows it often has strong differentiated competitive advantages in handling complex business scenarios in government services, financial services, and manufacturing, with few competitors.

Palantir's core products include Gotham, Foundry, Apollo, and AIP, with AIP built upon the company's mature data infrastructure. From a technical architecture perspective, excluding Apollo's operational automation capabilities and government clients' unique security compliance advantages, Foundry's strategic positioning can be defined as an enterprise-grade data operating platform, characterized by digital modeling of business entities and optimization of decision support systems through the Ontology framework.

The product is divided into three layers:

| Layer | Core Function | Technical Implementation |
|-------|---------------|-------------------------|
| Semantic | Unified data language, standardized entity relationships | Define object types (assets, transactions), attribute and link type mapping |
| Kinetic | Business logic dynamization and real-time response | Rule engine embedding in Ontology models (e.g., Drools), modular configuration of industry rules |
| Dynamic | Real-time optimization and AI collaborative decision-making | Machine learning dynamic data flow analysis, simulation and reasoning systems |

From a technical architecture perspective, the semantic layer effectively achieves logical unification of business data, completing data standardization. This technical path is typical in the internet industry's data middle platform construction methodology. What deserves deeper technical evaluation is the Kinetic Level design.

From existing case analysis, a considerable proportion of enterprise efficiency improvement needs stem from lagging informationization construction. The optimization effects in individual scenarios are difficult to accurately attribute, making it impossible to distinguish AI's actual contribution from natural gains in basic informationization construction. It's like using skincare products while simultaneously introducing variables like regular sleep and face cleaning—it becomes difficult to distinguish the skincare product's contribution from the combined effects of regular habits and deep cleaning. Palantir's core competitive advantage lies in its cross-industry solution integration capability, giving it significant differentiated advantages in helping enterprises achieve informationization upgrades. Excluding AI capability barriers, Palantir still possesses strong competitiveness.

## Part II: Commercial Barriers: Leading by Delivering Project Outcomes

Palantir received early investment from CIA's In-Q-Tel, becoming a core supplier for government counter-terrorism and intelligence analysis. Its technology was applied to critical missions including tracking Osama bin Laden. In 2024, government business revenue reached 55%, with clients including the Department of Defense and FBI, with long-term cooperation forming extremely high trust barriers. This is difficult for other industries to replicate. Let's focus on the remaining two points:

- **Frontline Deployment Engineer (FDSE) Model**: Palantir dispatches engineers to clients, deeply participating in data integration and decision processes, providing "software + consulting" hybrid services.

- **Ontology SDK-Driven Modular Transformation**: Integrating data, logic, and actions into standardized modules through Ontology (such as object types and link types), supporting rapid construction of AI applications. This enables the platform to flexibly adapt to 90 industries, such as financial fraud detection and medical data analysis. In 2024, commercial client count increased by 29% year-over-year.

In the market environment where AI technology leaders frequently face management questions about business ROI, adopting a complete project delivery model has become an effective solution. However, this model varies significantly across industries, with innovative drug fields being particularly notable. Worth attention is that when AIDD SaaS companies can provide complete project delivery capabilities, this triggers rethinking of the industry value chain: if third-party service providers can undertake core R&D functions, pharmaceutical companies' core competitive advantages will face restructuring. This topic was also raised during my business exchanges with domestic leading laboratory automation equipment suppliers: to promote automation experimental equipment, some suppliers have already formed early drug R&D teams to demonstrate how automation technology improves R&D efficiency through actual cases.

Following this commercial logic, can entrepreneurs directly copy the Palantir model to AIDD? This proposition had embryonic forms in the 2000 internet+ healthcare entrepreneurship cycle. It should be noted that after removing AIP (AI Platform) elements, Palantir is essentially scaled application of digital solutions in manufacturing scenarios. Theoretically, pharmaceutical industry digital transformation should have completed phase-based infiltration. Empirical data shows that multinational pharmaceutical companies (MNCs) have established comparative advantages in early R&D through automated laboratory systems for over twenty years, but this advantage shows diminishing returns given the long-cycle, multi-step characteristics of the biomedical industry. Notably, the iteration rates of molecular biology and digital technology between 2000-2025 show significant differences. The synergistic development of these two high-dynamic technology fields faces commercial落地 adaptation challenges, and mature solutions may not exist in terms of human resources or management methods.

Let's return to the wealth creation effect in the AI programming field—as they say, when one path ends, another begins. In their practical guide to programming agents, the Cognition team, summarizing two years of empirical research building Devin, proposed constructive advice: avoid over-optimization in agent middleware development, recommend integrated processing architecture, and maintain reasonable fault tolerance thresholds. This development strategy can enable engineering teams to maintain R&D efficiency and competitive advantages during technology iteration cycles.

From the technology evolution path perspective, the AIDD field may be more suitable for this highly simplified paradigm architecture. Although this is not the third-level "Dynamic" in the Foundry framework, it happens to also be a kind of Dynamic. It should be noted that this model imposes extremely high professional quality requirements on AIDD practitioners, essentially being a professional deepening process of "adding first then subtracting." But as people often say: people in investment focus on choice, people in industry focus on persistence. I believe AIDD practitioners' persistence will ultimately transform the industry.
