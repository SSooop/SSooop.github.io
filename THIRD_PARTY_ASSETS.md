# Third-Party Assets

This repository contains a mixture of original assets and third-party material used in a personal,
editorial website. The repository license does not apply uniformly to every image or other asset.

## How attribution is recorded

- Site-wide visual assets are documented in [`public/images/site/README.md`](./public/images/site/README.md).
- Article images record their source and credit in the article frontmatter or the nearby MDX image
  component, typically through fields such as `imageSource`, `imageSourceUrl`, `source`, and
  `sourceUrl`.
- A file-specific notice takes precedence over this general document.

## Rights boundary

Third-party images, screenshots, quotations, data, trademarks, and similar material are excluded
from the PolyForm license that covers repository software. They are also excluded from Alex Su's
copyright claim unless ownership is expressly stated.

An attribution or source URL documents provenance; it is not a sublicense. A local copy does not
grant repository visitors a general right to download, redistribute, modify, train on, or use the
material commercially. Anyone wishing to reuse an asset must review the original source terms and
obtain any permission required for that particular use.

Some material may be available under a stated license or public-domain designation. Other material
may be included for editorial quotation, commentary, criticism, or research under rights that are
specific to this site's context. Those grounds may not apply to downstream reuse.

## Corrections and removal requests

If you are a rights holder and believe a credit, usage-basis statement, or included asset is
incorrect, contact the author through the repository's public contact channels with the affected
file or page URL. The attribution will be corrected or the material removed when appropriate.
